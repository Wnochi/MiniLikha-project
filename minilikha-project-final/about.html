About the business

A hobby project with a polished shop experience.

MiniLikha began as a small class project and grew into a modern storefront concept for handmade, charming, and student-curated products.

Our story

Designed by classmates, shaped like a real brand.

Our shop started as a simple hobby project between classmates who wanted to build a clean and aesthetic online shopping experience. What began as practice became a way to learn branding, product presentation, customer flow, and responsive web design.

Every section of MiniLikha is built around a cute but professional feeling: soft colors, clear product browsing, smooth interactions, and a storefront that feels friendly from the first click.

Student-built branding

Logo, tone, layout, and content are shaped to feel simple, bright, and memorable.

Responsive shopping flow

The experience is planned for phones first, then refined for tablets and desktops.

Creative coding practice

Built to improve frontend skills while keeping the final page useful and delightful.
